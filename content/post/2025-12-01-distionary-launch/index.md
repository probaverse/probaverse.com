The core probaverse package, _distionary_, has been published to CRAN!
It defines the "probability distribution" object and provides infrastructure for their evaluation.
Learn more on the [blog post on the rOpenSci website](https://ropensci.org/blog/2026/01/20/introducing-distionary/).

This project has been five years in the making, and is just getting started.
Pretty soon, distionary will explicitly support discrete and mixed distributions.
Following that, the roadmap includes multivariate distributions, and distribution "families" rather than single distributions.
